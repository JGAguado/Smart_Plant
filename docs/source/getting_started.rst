Getting started
===============

Take your Smart Plant from the assembled board to its first readings.

1. Read :doc:`safety`. Check the battery connector polarity before applying power.
2. Follow :doc:`assembly` to connect the supported display and fit the enclosure.
3. Follow :doc:`programming` to configure Wi-Fi, flash ESPHome and verify one
   measurement, display refresh and sleep cycle.
4. Follow :doc:`labels` to give your plant its own display background.
5. Use :doc:`faq` if flashing, readings or connectivity do not work as expected.

Start with the supplied example for one device. After the first successful
cycle, choose whether you need Home Assistant and whether sleeping devices
should retain their readings there. The programming guide explains the API
and MQTT choices.
